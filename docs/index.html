Fluid mechanics and heat transfer ANSYS Fluent / CFX alternatives

OpenFOAM Foundation is a C++ finite-volume toolbox for internal and external flows, turbulence and conjugate heat transfer. SU2 is a C++ CFD suite with adjoint-based design tools for differentiable objectives such as drag and pressure losses. For enclosure and duct work, start with OpenFOAM and ParaView. Consider SU2 when geometry sensitivities are central to the workflow. Keep OpenFOAM Foundation and OpenCFD releases paired with their own tutorials, dictionaries and command syntax. What was measured

A five-point Jacobi sweep on fixed-boundary float64 buffers, using one CPU thread. All implementations agreed at 10⁻¹⁴ absolute and relative tolerance. Timings exclude buffer setup, compilation, and correctness checks. The C measurement includes one Python-to-C call per batch.

Intel Xeon Platinum 8573C, x86-64 Linux, CPython 3.12.14, NumPy 2.3.5 and GCC 14.2. The C kernel fuses operations in one pass, while these NumPy slices perform several passes. The ratio varies with cache, shape, compiler, and implementation. A large BLAS operation may call compiled kernels from either language.

Numba · JAX timing · Kokkos · PyO3 · Julia SciML

How to benchmark an industrial workload. Compare the same grid, physics, precision, tolerances and output error. Record preprocessing, solve and postprocessing separately, then measure end-to-end time, peak memory, throughput and cost. Test strong scaling at fixed problem size and weak scaling at fixed work per rank. Avoid oversubscribing BLAS/OpenMP threads inside MPI ranks. Sweep many independent cases in parallel before trying to distribute each solve. Examine sparse access, halo exchange and GPU transfer costs alongside FLOP counts. For this stencil, the work is O(N·S) and memory is O(N) for N grid cells and S sweeps. These bounds are shared by all three implementations.

Fastest pilot with facility data

Industrial pump and fan energy

Who buys it. plant energy managers, facilities teams and energy service companies. Fit pump and system curves to flow, head and power data. Compare throttling, variable speed control and pump staging over the measured duty cycle.

How to verify value. measured kWh per delivered unit, minimum service pressure and stable operation. Include maintenance costs and normalize for production. DOE publishes pump assessment resources.

Repeatable software deployment

Fleet-depot charging

Who buys it. fleet operators and charging integrators. Solve an LP or MILP for charging power, departure readiness, site capacity and utility tariff, then update on a rolling horizon.

How to verify value. peak kW, cost from the real tariff, and on-time departure charge. Replay historic arrivals, failed chargers and building-load spikes before enabling live control.

High-value engineering service

Data-center and enclosure cooling

Who buys it. colocation operators, liquid-cooling integrators and enclosure OEMs. Start with a calibrated thermal network. Run CFD on finalists to evaluate hot spots, bypass flows, pressure drop and fan or pump power.

How to verify value. worst credible inlet or component temperature, thermal margin during failures, and cooling energy at the same IT load. LBNL documents U.S. data-center energy growth scenarios. Revenue depends on pilot access and the value of reduced cooling energy or added capacity.

Power electronics and PCB test automation

Analog filter sizing

python filter.py
ngspice -b filter.cir

Enumerate 3,969 two-stage RC combinations, each at 16 independent tolerance corners. Require at most 0.5 dB passband loss at 100 Hz and maximize 10 kHz attenuation. Nominal SPICE checks the analytic transfer function.

−0.449 dB at 100 Hz and −50.688 dB at 10 kHz in separate worst cases

The model assumes an ideal buffer and omits source/load impedance, op-amp bandwidth and noise. Replace the buffer model and check those effects for a real board.
