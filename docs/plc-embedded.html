Electrical, electronic, analog, and digital

Electrical, electronic, and computer engineering describe domains of work. Analog and digital describe how signals or computations are represented. A PLC cabinet and a microcontroller board will often handle both kinds of signal.

LayerPLC applicationsEmbedded applications
Engineering scopeIndustrial electrical engineering, automation, instrumentation, and control. Engineers coordinate machinery, production lines, and processes, and maintain I/O lists, interlocks, alarms, HMIs, and fieldbuses.Firmware is typically embedded software or computer engineering. Board design is electronics engineering. Motor and converter control also involve control engineering and power electronics.
Digital logic and executionTasks may run periodically or in response to events. A cyclic task commonly reads the process image, runs the sequence logic, and updates the outputs. IEC 61131-3 includes Structured Text (ST), Ladder Diagram (LD), and Function Block Diagram (FBD).MCUs, SoCs, or dedicated controllers run firmware, often written in C or C++. GPIO, timers, PWM, DMA, interrupt service routines, interrupt priorities, and the RTOS shape event handling and timing.

The cabinet also interfaces with sensors, relays, drives, and power supplies.Board-level electronics cover signal conditioning, anti-aliasing, ADCs, DACs, voltage references, and power design. Firmware processes discrete samples and accounts for quantization, overflow, and the timebase.

Two experiments show what simulation can predict

Both exercises use synthetic inputs. One isolates sampling phase, and the other quantifies PI tuning trade-offs across operating conditions.

Will the PLC detect a short pulse?

Assume a fixed scan interval and instantaneous input sampling with no filtering. The pulse starts at a whole-millisecond offset and goes low at the end of its specified width. The PLC detects it if a sample falls inside the high interval.

Detected 6 of 10 phases (4 missed). Maximum wait among detections is 5 ms.
6 / 10

These results describe phase coverage. Real events can be correlated with the machine cycle. Measure the missed-event rate on the actual system.

Turn missed pulses into a test case

In a virtual loading, inspection, and unloading exercise, a sensor may emit a 6 ms pulse as a pallet passes. If inputs are sampled every 10 ms, testing only a pulse that starts exactly at the scan boundary gives an overly optimistic result.

Sweep the phase. Use pallet IDs and a request/acknowledge handshake to carry events across stations. Evaluate shortening the poll interval, using a hardware latch or high-speed counter where supported, or holding the event upstream until the next station acknowledges it. Record delay, lost counts, duplicate acknowledgments, and restart behavior for each option.

Measure end-to-end delay using timestamps at the physical input and output. The process image has its own update timing, and input modules can filter the signal first. High-priority tasks, the network, and output modules can also affect response time. Siemens response time manual

Check PI tuning beyond nominal conditions

For a first-order thermal system C dT/dt = P u − H(T − Tₐ), hold the output u ∈ [0,1] constant within each one-second step. Define scenarios by thermal capacity, heat-loss coefficient, heater power, ambient temperature, and sensor bias and lag. Each simulation runs for 300 s with a 38 °C setpoint and the same added heat-loss disturbance. The controller uses the simulated sensor reading. Calculate tracking error and overshoot from the model's true temperature.

Enumerate 25 sets of PI gains. The nominal method uses the baseline case alone. The scenario-based method requires all three training cases to run without faults and with overshoot ≤ 2 °C, then minimizes the largest cost across those cases. The cost is integrated absolute temperature error plus modeled energy weighted by 0.002 °C·s/J. The weight can be adjusted for the application.

MethodKp / KiOvershoot in warm case with a slow sensorMaximum training costOvershoot in holdout case
Nominal optimum0.20 / 0.0093.122 °C, above the limit784.42 °C·s1.419 °C
Multi-scenario minimax0.12 / 0.00251.827 °C, within the limit909.26 °C·s1.416 °C

Five of the 25 gain sets satisfy all three training cases. The scenario-based method reduces overshoot in the selected conditions at a higher worst-case tracking and energy cost. A single synthetic holdout case provides an additional check. Different parameter ranges, unmodeled faults, or field data can change the result.

What can hardware tests reveal that simulation misses?

Models can predict the mechanisms and operating limits they represent. Unmodeled interactions, incorrect initial conditions, or insufficient measurement bandwidth can cause a discrepancy. When you see one, locate where it appears in the chain before deciding whether to change the model.

Source of discrepancyTypical observationHow to investigate
Parameters and operating conditionsSensor bias drifts with temperature, or load, heat loss, or friction differs from the nominal value.Record operating conditions and equipment batches, repeat tests, and validate on independent conditions. Consider updating the model if residuals show a consistent pattern across conditions. Use ranges or corner cases when there is little evidence for a probability distribution.
Omitted physical mechanismsActuator dead zones, saturation, backlash, thermal gradients, supply dips, or resets caused by switching interference.Log commands and physical feedback at the same time and align timestamps. Isolate power supply, load, filtering, and mechanical changes with controlled A/B tests.
Timing, protocols, and softwareOccasionally missed pulses, dropped frames, stale data after reconnecting, task preemption, or race conditions.Inject events across phases and reproduce intermittent communications and restarts. Use sequence numbers and freshness checks to find missing, duplicate, and stale events. Report sample counts, tail latency, and the maximum observed delay.
Measurement chain and numerical errorScope sampling or probing affects the waveform, or the ADC reference or calibration introduces bias. Simulation step size can also affect peaks.Record instruments, bandwidth, synchronization, and calibration. Estimate repeatability and systematic effects, and compare against independent instruments. Check simulation step size, numerical tolerances, and units. NIST measurement process handbook

Nonlinearity is a system property that can be modeled, for example with saturation or hysteresis. Uncertainty can also arise in a deterministic linear model, such as when thermal capacity is imprecisely known. A Monte Carlo study propagates the specified input distributions. Its coverage depends on the assumptions and data. Use observation, fault injection, or a more representative test bench to discover mechanisms missing from the model.

Use the same acceptance test from simulation to hardware

  1. Define states, boundaries, inputs, outputs, and thresholds in the offline model. Use deterministic replay to check minimum pulse width, blocking, the first cause of a fault, and conditions for safe recovery.
  2. Use software-in-the-loop (SIL) to check the PLC program or firmware in a software environment. Use hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) to connect the real controller to controlled I/O or a real-time plant simulator so that the test includes task timing and interfaces.
  3. On a controlled bench, record the physical input, controller sample, control output, and actuator feedback. Record hardware and software versions. Use one set of conditions for identification and another for validation. Keep raw logs, measurement uncertainty, failed test cases, and results of the repeat tests after fixes.

A timing guarantee requires defined conditions, adequate coverage, and timing analysis. Report a measured maximum as the largest value observed in your tests. NIST guidance on measurement uncertainty

电气、电子、模拟、数字怎么分

电气／电子／计算机工程描述工程对象和工作内容。模拟／数字描述信号或计算的实现方式。一个 PLC 控制柜或一块 MCU 板通常会同时涉及两类信号。

短脉冲能被 PLC 看到吗？

设扫描固定，输入采样瞬时且无滤波。脉冲从任一整数毫秒相位开始，结束时回低。扫描恰好落在高电平区间才算命中。

这是相位覆盖结果。真实事件到达可能和设备周期相关，现场漏采率需要实测。

把漏采变成测试

在上料 → 检测 → 下料的虚拟练习中，传感器可能只在托盘经过时发出 6 ms 脉冲。若输入采样间隔 10 ms，测试只把信号放在扫描开始就会得到过于乐观的结果。

逐相位扫脉冲，跨工位用托盘 ID 和请求／确认保持事件。缩短轮询周期、按设备支持选硬件锁存或高速计数，或由上游保持事件直到下游确认。把各方法的延迟、计数丢失、重复确认和重启恢复放进同一张测试表。

用输入与输出端的实测时间戳检验端到端延迟。过程映像有刷新时序，输入模块可能先做滤波。高优先级任务、网络与输出模块也可能改变响应。Siemens 响应时间手册

PI 整定要在偏离名义条件时复核

对一阶热系统 C dT/dt = P u − H(T − Tₐ)，在每秒保持输出 u ∈ [0,1]，将热容、散热系数、功率、环境温度、传感器偏置与滞后作为工况输入。模型用 300 s 运行、38 °C 设定值和相同的额外散热扰动。控制器反馈来自模拟传感器。跟踪误差和超调按模型真实温度计算。

枚举 25 组 PI 参数。名义法只用基准工况；情景法要求三个训练工况均无故障且超调 ≤ 2 °C，再最小化训练工况中的最大代价。代价为绝对温差积分加上系数 0.002 °C·s/J 乘模型能量，系数可按应用目标重设。

方案Kp / Ki暖环境、慢传感器超调最大训练代价保留工况超调

多情景最坏值优化0.12 / 0.00251.827 °C，满足约束909.26 °C·s1.416 °C

25 组中 5 组对三个训练工况都可行。情景法为减少所选工况的超调，接受了更高的最坏跟踪与能量代价。单个未参与训练的合成工况提供额外的复核点。不同参数范围、未建模故障和现场数据都可能改变结论。

实测会出现仿真没预测到的现象吗？

会。模型可以提前预见已考虑的机制和边界。未知的耦合、错误的初始条件或不足的测量带宽，可能让结果偏离仿真。遇到偏差时先定位哪一段出现差异，再决定是否改模型。

偏差来源典型现场表现怎么验证或缩小范围
参数与外部工况传感器偏置随温度漂移；负载、散热或摩擦与名义值不同。记录工况和设备批次，重复试验并用独立工况验证。不同工况下若偏差有结构，考虑更新模型；缺少分布证据时用区间或角点。
被省略的物理机制执行器死区、饱和、齿隙、热梯度、供电跌落或开关干扰引起复位。同时记录指令与物理反馈，并对准时间戳。用受控 A/B 实验隔离供电、负载、滤波或机械变化。
时序、协议与软件偶发脉冲漏采、丢帧、重连后的旧数据、任务抢占或并发竞态。逐相位注入事件，复现断续通讯及重启，用序号和新鲜度检查重复、丢失和过期。量延迟分布的尾部与观测到的最大延迟，并报告样本数。
测量链与数值误差示波器采样或探测条件改变波形，ADC 参考或标定产生偏差；仿真步长也能改变峰值。记录仪器、带宽、同步与校准，量重复性和系统性影响，比较独立仪器。对仿真做步长、容差与单位检查。NIST 测量过程

非线性是可建模的系统性质，饱和与回差就是例子。不确定性也可出现在确定的线性模型中，例如热容参数不精确。蒙特卡洛只传播选定输入的概率模型。它覆盖的范围取决于假设和数据，未建模的故障需要通过观测、故障注入或更接近实际的台架寻找。

从仿真到硬件，保留同一条验收用例

  1. 在离线模型中写清状态、边界、输入、输出和阈值。用确定性回放测试最小脉宽、阻塞、故障首因和安全恢复条件。
  2. 用软件在环（SIL）验证模拟 PLC 或固件的实现。用硬件在环（HIL）连接真实控制器与受控 I/O 或实时装置模拟器，把任务时序和接口拉进测试范围。
  3. 用受控台架记录物理输入、控制器采样、控制输出和执行器反馈。锁定软硬件版本，用一部分工况辨识、另一部分做验证。保留原始日志、测量不确定度、失败用例和改善后的复测。
